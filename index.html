Red Car

Out now · a taxi delivery world with a twist

Download Red Car

A little red truck, a whole town to cross, and the clock always ticking. Pick up passengers and parcels, from Granny Snail to A Very Large Chicken to a cake you must not tilt, and drop them off before time runs out. Then the Gloop factions start feuding, someone asks you to fetch candles, chalk and a lot of cats, and something very big climbs out of a portal behind the Church. Eight chapters, from your first delivery to a rather unusual ending.
